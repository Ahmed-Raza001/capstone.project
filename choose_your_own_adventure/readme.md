# 🗺️ Choose Your Own Adventure Game

Welcome to **Choose Your Own Adventure**, a simple text-based adventure game built using Python.

In this game, you become the main character and make decisions that determine your fate. Every choice takes you on a different path. Choose wisely because one wrong decision could end your adventure!

## 🎮 Game Overview

The game starts by asking for your name. You then find yourself on a dirt road where you must choose between going left or right.

Depending on your decisions, you may:
- 🌊 Swim across a river and encounter an alligator.
- 🚶 Walk around a river but run out of water.
- 🌉 Cross a shaky bridge.
- 🏆 Meet a stranger who rewards you with gold.
- 💀 Make the wrong decision and lose the game.

Your goal is to make the right choices and win the adventure!

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Python 3**
- **VS Code** (Code Editor)

## 📚 Python Concepts Used

This project helped me practice the following Python concepts:

- `input()` – Taking input from the user.
- `print()` – Displaying messages.
- Variables – Storing user input.
- `if`, `elif`, and `else` – Making decisions.
- Nested conditional statements – Handling multiple choices inside other conditions.
- `lower()` – Converting user input to lowercase.
- String concatenation and formatting.
